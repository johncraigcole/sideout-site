# Sideout Help

Sideout is two iPhone apps that work together to film volleyball games:

- **SideoutCam** runs on the phone on the tripod. It records the game with a live scoreboard in the video, cuts highlight clips, streams live, and uploads your games to Google Drive and YouTube.
- **SideoutTally** runs on the phone in your hand. It keeps score, starts and stops the recording, marks highlights and shows you what the camera sees, from anywhere in the gym.

You can use SideoutCam on its own, but the two together are the way it's meant to be used: set the camera up once, then run everything from your seat.

## A typical game

1. Mount the camera phone, open SideoutCam, and open SideoutTally on your phone. They connect on their own once you've paired them.
2. On either phone, tap **Start Game**, pick the opponent, and tap **Start Game** again. The camera screen opens.
3. Tap **Start Recording** when the set begins. Tap a team's score for every point.
4. Tap **★ Highlight** right after a great play.
5. When the set ends, tap **Stop**, then **End Set**. Record the next set the same way.
6. After the game, open **Games** on the camera phone and tap **Upload**.
