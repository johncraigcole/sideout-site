# Getting Started

## What you need

- **Two iPhones.** One for the camera (SideoutCam, iOS 17 or later) and one for your hand (SideoutTally, iOS 16 or later). A newer phone makes the better camera.
- **A tripod or mount** that holds the camera phone sideways (landscape).
- **A battery pack** for long days. Recording with the screen on uses roughly 20–30% of the battery an hour.
- **No Wi-Fi network needed.** The two phones talk to each other directly, so it works in any gym.

## First launch on the camera phone

Open **SideoutCam**. When asked, allow:

- **Camera**: to record.
- **Microphone**: for game sound. Without it, videos are silent.
- **Local Network**: so SideoutTally can find the camera.
- **Photos** (optional): only if you want copies of recordings in your photo library.

Then set up your team:

1. Tap the **Filming for** card at the top of the home screen ▸ **Edit My Teams…**.
2. Your first team is already there. Tap it, give it a name (e.g. "Westview Jr. Boys") and pick its **Scoreboard team**: the name, colors and logo shown on the scoreboard. If it isn't in the list yet, add it first under **Teams** on the home screen (see [Teams and Opponents](03-Teams-and-Opponents.md)).

That's enough to record. Uploads and streaming can be set up later ([My Teams and Accounts](02-My-Teams-and-Accounts.md)).

## Connecting SideoutTally

1. On both phones, turn **Wi-Fi on** in Control Center. The phones don't need to join a network, but Wi-Fi must be switched on. With Wi-Fi off, they fall back to Bluetooth only, which is very slow.
2. Open **SideoutCam** on the camera phone. Its home screen shows a 4-digit **pairing code**.
3. Open **SideoutTally** and tap **Search for Camera**. Pick the camera phone from **Nearby cameras** and enter the code.

From then on, SideoutTally remembers the camera. Tap **Reconnect to SideoutCam** and it connects without the code.

> Tip: SideoutTally only searches when you tap Search or Reconnect, to save battery. If the phones lose each other (you walked out of range, or locked the camera phone), tap **Reconnect**.

## Keeping the camera phone awake

SideoutCam keeps the camera phone's screen on while the home screen or camera screen is open, so it won't lock in the middle of a game. If you press the side button or leave the app, the camera stops: iPhone apps can't record in the background.
