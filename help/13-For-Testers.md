# For Testers

Thanks for trying Sideout! It's still being built, so you'll get test versions through Apple's **TestFlight** app before anything is in the App Store. This page covers getting set up, what to try, and how to tell us what you find.

## What you need

- **Two iPhones** for the full setup: one on a tripod as the camera (**SideoutCam**, iOS 17 or later), and one in your hand as the remote (**SideoutTally**, iOS 16 or later). An older iPhone you don't use any more makes a great remote. SideoutCam also works on its own with just the camera phone.
- **A tripod or mount** that holds the camera phone sideways.
- **Wi-Fi switched on** on both phones (Control Center). They don't need to join a network: the two phones talk to each other directly, so it works in any gym.
- **A charger or battery pack** for the camera phone on long days.

## Installing

1. On each phone, install Apple's free **TestFlight** app from the App Store.
2. Open the **invitation link** you were sent, on each phone. It opens TestFlight.
3. Install **SideoutCam** on the camera phone and **SideoutTally** on the remote phone. You can install both on both phones; you'll just use one on each.
4. New versions show up in TestFlight. Turn on **Automatic Updates** there, or tap **Update** when you see one. Each test version works for 90 days; there's always a newer one before then.

## First steps

1. Open **SideoutCam** on the camera phone and allow the **Camera**, **Microphone** and **Local Network** access it asks for.
2. Set up your team: **My Teams** (under More) ▸ your team ▸ give it a name and a **Scoreboard team** (its name, colors and logo on the scoreboard). [Getting Started](01-Getting-Started.md) walks through it.
3. Connect the remote: open **SideoutTally**, tap **Search for Camera**, pick the camera phone and type the 4-digit code shown on SideoutCam's home screen. After that it remembers the camera.
4. Start a game from either phone (**Start Game**), tap **Start Recording** when the set begins, and score from the remote. [Game Day](04-Game-Day.md) has the details.

A practice or a scrimmage is a good first try: there's no pressure, and you can try everything.

## Google Drive and YouTube

Uploading to Google Drive and YouTube, and going live on YouTube through your account, need a Google sign-in, which is still in testing with Google. If you'd like to try it, **send the email address of your Google account** to the address below and we'll add it. Google asks testers to sign in again about once a week.

Everything else works without signing in to anything: recording with the scoreboard, highlights, the remote, sponsors, player reels, and sharing videos by AirDrop or Messages. Streaming to Facebook or another service with a stream key doesn't need Google either.

## What to try

- A full game: recording each set, scoring from the remote, ending sets, **★ Highlight** after great plays, and tagging who made them.
- The remote's **camera view** to check the framing from your seat.
- **Games** on the camera phone afterwards: play a set, **Clip** a play you missed, **Share** a highlight.
- A **player reel** for one player (**Player Reels**, under More).
- If you stream: **Go Live**, pausing for a timeout, and the stream cards between sets.

## Sending feedback

- **From TestFlight:** take a screenshot in SideoutCam or SideoutTally and tap **Share Beta Feedback**, or open TestFlight ▸ the app ▸ **Send Beta Feedback**. Your message and screenshot come straight to us with the app version and phone model.
- **If an app crashes,** TestFlight asks whether to send a report. Please say yes and add a line about what you were doing.
- **By email:** sideoutcamapp@gmail.com.

The most useful reports say what you tapped, what you expected and what happened, and roughly when (so we can match it up). For connection problems between the phones, say how far apart they were and whether it was in a gym.

## Your videos and privacy

Your games stay on your camera phone unless you upload or share them. Uploads go to **your own** Google Drive and YouTube, never to us. TestFlight shares only what you choose to send, plus crash reports if you allow them. See the [privacy policy](https://sideoutcam.com/privacy).
