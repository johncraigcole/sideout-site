# Sponsors

Show up to ten sponsors in the bottom-right corner of game recordings and live streams. They take turns, with a short fade between them.

Each of My Teams has its own sponsors: **My Teams ▸ the team ▸ Sponsors**.

## Adding sponsors

- **Add Sponsor Image**: pick a logo from Photos. It's shown on a white card, sized to the logo's shape: wide logos are wide and short, while square and tall logos get more height, so each reads at about the same size. A card is never taller than the scoreboard. Tap an image sponsor to change its **card color**: **White** (the default), **Black** (best for a logo with white lettering), **Team** (the team's scoreboard color) or **Custom** (any color). The preview shows it as it will look in the video.
- **Add Text Sponsor**: up to two lines of text, with a choice of font, text color and card color. Handy when you don't have a logo.

Drag to reorder. Choose the **Turn length**, how long each sponsor shows (5, 10, 15 or 30 seconds), and use **Show sponsors in recordings** to turn them all off without deleting them.

## Sponsor tiers

If your sponsors come in levels, tap the **No Tier** button at the end of a sponsor's row and pick **Platinum**, **Gold**, **Silver** or **Bronze**.

- The card gets a thin edge in the tier's color and a small dark tab on top that says, for example, **GOLD SPONSOR**.
- The list keeps sponsors in tier order, Platinum first, and they take turns in that order.
- **Platinum** sponsors show twice as long as the turn length, and **Gold** 1.5 times as long. **Silver** and sponsors without a tier show for the turn length. The Turn length choices list their times too, such as "10 seconds · Gold 15, Platinum 20".
- **Bronze** sponsors show only twice in each set: once near the start, and again about 5 minutes in. The warm-up counts as its own set.
- Sponsors without a tier look and show just as before.

## Good to know

- Sponsors are part of the video itself, just like the scoreboard. They can't be removed from a recording afterwards, so set them up before you record.
- They appear in **game sets, warm-ups and highlights**, and on the **live stream**, but not on stream-only cards ("Waiting for Set 2") and never in practice recordings.
- The camera screen shows them exactly where and when they'll appear in the video.
