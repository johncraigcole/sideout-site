# Practice Recordings and Scrimmages

## Practice recordings

Use **Practice** for anything that isn't a game or a scrimmage: drills, tryouts, a skills video. (On SideoutTally the button is **Practice Recording**.)

1. Tap **Practice** on the camera phone, or **Practice Recording** on SideoutTally.
2. If you have more than one of My Teams, pick which team it's for (it uploads to that team's Drive folder and playlist).
3. Name it, e.g. "Serving drills", and tap **Start Recording**. Recording starts as soon as the camera is ready.

Practice recordings:

- have **no scoreboard** and no sponsors;
- can have **highlights**;
- are named like `20261004-Serving-drills.mov`, and recording again with the same name on the same day continues the same practice;
- upload to a folder like "20261004 Serving drills" on Google Drive.

Stop with **Stop**, and finish with **⋯ ▸ End Practice** (SideoutTally) or the **✕** on the camera screen.

## Scrimmages

A scrimmage splits your team's roster into two sides (Team A vs. Team B) and scores it like a game, with the scoreboard on the video.

1. Tap **Scrimmage** on either phone. On the camera phone it's for the team shown under **Filming for**; on SideoutTally, pick the team if you have more than one.
2. Pick the sides, one of two ways (the switch at the top; your choice is remembered):
   - **Fill one side:** everyone starts on Team B. Tap players to put them on Team A; everyone else plays for Team B. Tap a Team A player again to send them back.
   - **Captains pick:** everyone starts in a pool, and each tap gives the next player to the side whose pick it is (Team A, Team B, Team A…), as captains taking turns. Choose which side picks first; **Undo Pick** takes back a mis-tap; **Rest to Smaller Side** (or just tapping Start) puts whoever's left on the side with fewer players.
   - **Same as Last Time** brings back the last scrimmage's split. **Shuffle** makes a random, even split of everyone playing. **Clear** starts over.
   - Press and hold a player and tap **Sit Out** for anyone not playing today; tap them under **Sitting out** to bring them back.
   - Rename the sides if you like ("Blue" and "White", up to 10 characters).
3. Choose how each set ends: **Play on** (no target: the set ends whenever you tap **End Set**, even on a tied score, which then counts for neither side), **To 15** or **To 25** (win by 2).
4. Tap **Start**. The camera opens with the scoreboard. Tap **Record** when play starts.

The split (for Same as Last Time), the side names and the target are remembered for that team's next scrimmage.

During a scrimmage:

- Score, **End Set**, **Pause**, **Highlight** and stats work as in a game. There's no limit on sets: keep going until you end the scrimmage. The scoreboard shows the last four sets.
- **Sides** (under End Set on SideoutTally, or in the camera's score controls) shows who's on which side: tap a player to move them across, or hold to sit them out.
- When tagging a highlight, pick the side, then the player.
- **Stats** list both sides' players. A stat that ends the rally scores for the side the player is on: a Kill by a Team B player is a point for Team B.
- There are no lineups or warm-ups in a scrimmage.
- **Go Live** works; viewers see "Scrimmage: Team A vs. Team B" on the cards.

When you upload the scrimmage to Google Drive, its **Game Summary** lists the **Teams** (who was on each side, and who sat out), and notes **Sides changed** under any set where players had moved.

Finish with **⋯ ▸ End Scrimmage** (SideoutTally) or the **✕** on the camera screen: **Save Score & End** keeps the score and sides to resume later; **End Scrimmage** finishes it. If the app closes unexpectedly, the scrimmage can be resumed from the home screen. (If you have a game saved to resume later, a scrimmage leaves it alone and isn't saved itself.)

Scrimmages:

- are named like `20261007-Scrimmage-TeamA-TeamB-Set-1.mov`, with highlights like `20261007-Scrimmage-TeamA-TeamB-Set-1-Parker-Kill.mov`;
- are listed in **Games** marked "Scrimmage", under the team they were filmed for;
- upload to a folder like "20261007 Scrimmage Team A vs. Team B" on Google Drive (never in a tournament folder), with a highlight reel and Game Summary like games;
- are **not** included in player reels.
