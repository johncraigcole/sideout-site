# Highlights

By the time you know a play was great, it's already over. That's why a highlight clips the seconds **before** you tap.

## Marking a highlight

Tap **★ Highlight** (SideoutTally) or **Highlight** (camera screen) right after the play, while recording. A tag sheet opens on that phone:

- **Clip length**: 10, 15, 20, 30 or 60 seconds before the tap (plus 2 seconds after). Your last choice is remembered.
- **Team**: which team made the play.
- **Player**: a player from that team's roster, or the whole team (e.g. "Westview (team)"). If you've set a lineup, the players on court (and liberos) appear as cards: tap the one who made the play. Anyone else, or the whole team, is in the dropdown underneath. Your players are added in [My Teams](02-My-Teams-and-Accounts.md), opponents' in [Teams](03-Teams-and-Opponents.md).
- **Play**: Kill, Ace, Block, Dig, Pass, Set, Tip, Save or Brilliance.
- **Note** (optional): a few words.

Tap **Save**, or **Skip** to keep the clip untagged. The recording keeps going the whole time.

## Where highlights go

When you tap **Stop**, each highlight is cut from the set's video and saved under **Games**, named from its tag, e.g. `20261004-Westview-Eastside-Set-2-Parker-Kill.mov`. Untagged clips are named `…-Highlight-01`, `…-Highlight-02`, and so on.

Highlights upload with the game: to a **Highlights** folder on Google Drive, and after the sets in your YouTube playlist.

Warm-ups don't have highlights. Practice recordings do.

Tagging your players also lets you make a **player reel**: one player's highlights from a game, a tournament or a season, as one video to keep or share. See [After the Game ▸ Player reels](09-After-the-Game.md#player-reels).
