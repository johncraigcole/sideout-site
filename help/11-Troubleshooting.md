# Troubleshooting

## The phones won't connect, or the connection is slow

- **Turn Wi-Fi on on both phones** (Control Center). They don't need to join a network, but with Wi-Fi off they use Bluetooth only, which is very slow and drops easily.
- **Open SideoutCam first**, on its home screen or camera screen. The camera phone must be awake and SideoutCam open. A locked phone can't be found.
- **Tap Reconnect** in SideoutTally. It only searches when asked, to save battery.
- **Stay within range.** About 20–30 m with a clear line of sight works well; walls and crowds shorten it.
- **"Code changed"?** The pairing code is on SideoutCam's home screen. Tap **Search for Camera** and enter it again.
- If all else fails, choose **Forget This Camera** in SideoutTally and pair again.

## The stream keeps showing "Weak signal" or reconnecting

The camera phone's upload connection is too slow or unsteady. Run a speed test at the camera spot: for 720p you want a steady 5 Mbps upload or more. Try mounting the phone higher or nearer a window or door, make sure 5G is on, or use 720p instead of 1080p. Viewers keep the same link while Sideout reconnects.

## No sound in my videos

SideoutCam needs microphone access: Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Microphone ▸ SideoutCam. The camera screen warns you when it's off.

## Go Live is grey

No destination is set up for the team you're filming for. Tap Go Live for a hint, then sign in to YouTube or add a stream key in **Accounts**, and pick it for the team in **My Teams** ([Live Streaming](07-Live-Streaming.md)).

## "Your YouTube sign-in expired" or "Your Google Drive sign-in expired"

Google sometimes asks you to sign in again, for example after you change your Google password or remove Sideout's access in your Google account. Go to **Accounts** and sign in again. Uploads continue where they left off.

## Upload stopped with "daily upload limit"

YouTube limits uploads per channel per day. Tap Upload again tomorrow; finished videos are skipped.

## The camera stopped when I pressed the side button

iPhone apps can't record in the background or with the screen locked. Everything up to that moment is saved. Leave SideoutCam open on the camera screen; it keeps the screen on by itself.

## "Recovered after an unexpected stop"

The camera phone shut down or SideoutCam closed while recording. Sideout saved everything up to about the last 10 seconds and added it back to Games, marked **recovered**. If it was a game, **Resume** on the home screen brings back the score too.

## The phone is getting hot

Fast charging, streaming over cellular, 60 fps or 4K, a bright screen, sun and a thick case all add heat. If the phone gets too hot, iOS may slow it down or stop the camera (everything recorded until then is kept).

Quick fixes: start at 100% and charge with a slow charger (or not at all), stream over Wi-Fi or another phone's hotspot, keep the remote connected so the screen dims, and when you're live and it's hot, use **Pause Stream to Cool Down…** in the warning. See [Keeping the Camera Phone Cool](12-Keeping-the-Phone-Cool.md) for the full picture, including why a 720p stream still leaves the phone working hard, and 30 vs. 60 fps.

## Running out of space

Sideout warns on both phones when there's about an hour of recording space left (orange) and when there's 20 minutes or less (red), at the quality you're recording in. It also warns when the camera phone's battery is at 20% or less and not charging, and when Low Power Mode is on (it can slow the phone).

Upload and delete finished games, or move them to an external drive. See [After the Game](09-After-the-Game.md).
