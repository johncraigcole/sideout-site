# Sponsors

Show up to five sponsors in the bottom-right corner of game recordings and live streams. They take turns, with a short fade between them.

Each of My Teams has its own sponsors: **Filming for ▸ Edit My Teams… ▸ the team ▸ Sponsors**.

## Adding sponsors

- **Add Sponsor Image**: pick a logo from Photos. It's shown on a white card.
- **Add Text Sponsor**: up to two lines of text, with a choice of font, text color and card color. Handy when you don't have a logo.

Drag to reorder. Choose how long each sponsor shows (5, 10, 15 or 30 seconds), and use **Show sponsors in recordings** to turn them all off without deleting them.

## Good to know

- Sponsors are part of the video itself, just like the scoreboard. They can't be removed from a recording afterwards, so set them up before you record.
- They appear in **game sets, warm-ups and highlights**, and on the **live stream**, but not on stream-only cards ("Waiting for Set 2") and never in practice recordings.
- The camera screen shows them exactly where and when they'll appear in the video.
